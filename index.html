Your account, your data

Sign in simply. Stay in control.

Here's exactly what happens when you create a Fantasy Forge account, and why.

Sign in your way

Create an account with your email address, or continue with Google or Apple. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, so Fantasy Forge never stores your password.

What Google sign-in shares

If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your basic profile: your name, email address and profile picture. We use it only to create your account, sign you in and confirm your email address.

We never ask for access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

Never sold. Delete any time.

Your data is never sold and never used for advertising. In game, other players know you by the username you choose.

You can permanently delete your account and content at any time from Profile → Account Settings.

Questions

Before you set out

What is Fantasy Forge?

Fantasy Forge is an app for creating, sharing and playing AI-powered, text-based role-playing adventures. You describe what your character does in your own words, and an AI Game Master narrates what happens. It illustrates the scenes, tracks your stats and inventory, and voices the characters you meet.

Is it free?

Yes. Fantasy Forge is free to download and play. Optional in-app purchases, like token packs and memberships, give extra AI turns and premium features to players who want more.

What can I play it on?

Android phones and tablets through Google Play, and any modern web browser at playfantasyforge.com.

Can I play with friends?

Absolutely. Host a lobby for up to five players, invite your friends and explore branching storylines together in real time. Everyone's actions shape the same story.

Can I make my own adventures?

Yes. In the game editor you write your premise, pick genres and an art style, create characters with stats and weapons, then lay out locations and key story events. When it's ready, publish it for the community to play. If you'd like a head start, the AI tools can draft a world for you.

How old do I need to be?

Fantasy Forge is intended for players aged 13 and over.

How is my data used, and how do I delete my account?

We collect only what's needed to run your account and your games. Everything is explained in plain language in our Privacy Policy. You can delete your account permanently in the app at any time. See how to delete your account.

Who makes Fantasy Forge?

Fantasy Forge is made by Hinz Apps, an independent developer in Brisbane, Australia. Questions, feedback and bug reports are always welcome at thatmewh@gmail.com.
